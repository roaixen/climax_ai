One operating picture

Three layers, one continuous loop

A forecast is only useful if it changes what happens on the ground. Climax connects the prediction to the response, and the response back to the model.

01 PREDICT

The hazard

Continuous forecasts across hazard families, refreshed as the data changes instead of once a day — weather, water, seismic, satellite and civic reports in one timeline, with provenance on every number.

02 PREPARE

The response

Exposure analysis by region and group, with the specific safety measures each one needs and when. Who is doing what, by when — shared between officials, responders and volunteers.

03 PARTICIPATE

The community

Communities act together, earn credits for it, and feed verified ground truth back into the model. Neighbours who help during an event earn credits they can spend on preparedness and services.

Questions, answered

Before you ask

Can it actually predict a disaster?

Hazard models run continuously on live feeds, so a warning exists while there is still time to act. Confidence ships with every prediction, and the feed behind a warning stays visible so officials can see why it fired.

Does this replace official warnings?

No. Agencies stay the authority. Climax adds lead time, local specificity and coordination; when an official warning is issued, we amplify it.

Where does the data come from?

Weather, water, seismic, satellite and civic reports in one timeline, with provenance on every number.

How do community credits work?

Neighbours who help during an event earn credits they can spend on preparedness and services. Credits are confirmed by the people helped and the coordinator on site — never by post count.

What happens to personal data?

We alert areas and groups, not dossiers of people. Alerts are scoped to areas and groups rather than individual histories. Pilot data stays with the pilot, is never sold, and can be deleted on request.

What does a pilot involve?

Six weeks in one region: feed integration, a shared risk picture for your teams, community onboarding, and an after-action report you keep.
