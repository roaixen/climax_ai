Climax - Environment & Civic AI Intelligence System/Application 

I am building a platform  & intelligence system that will predict common natural hazards, calamities before it happens and threats of events based on data sources available in the internet, satellites, electronics, & AI sensors on a real-time continuum. Also, it will report & analyze, responses to the sets of location and region that might have the highest chances of impact and to alert safety measures to the group of individuals/personals. 
Using all those data and instructions, will lead them to consult & use our services and with govt. Officials and provide assistances according to their needs and requirements.
Also, it will have a community platform where they can help each other and get its credits for it.
